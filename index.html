The Idea

A Beautiful System Error in Your Week.

Corporate routines run on continuous loops. Meetings, messages, targets, commutes — and then Monday starts again. The Glitch Chord is designed to interrupt that loop.

Every Sunday afternoon, a deliberately small circle of professionals gathers inside an intimate lounge to engage with live guitars, pianos and acoustic vocals. Sing along. Pick up an instrument. Listen quietly. There is no stage to impress and no crowd to perform for.

It is simply a room where ambitious people can put the work persona down for a few hours and reconnect with music, people and themselves.
